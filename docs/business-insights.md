# Business Insights

This document summarizes the main business readings from the dashboard.

## Executive Overview

The marketplace shows a strong transactional base, with approximately 100k orders and a large customer footprint.

Key readings:

- The business is strongly concentrated in a few high-performing categories.
- Customer demand is geographically concentrated in large Brazilian states.
- Delivery performance is a central driver for customer experience.
- Review score is generally positive, but low-score reviews reveal important operational risks.

## Sales And Categories

Top categories by GMV include:

- health and beauty
- watches and gifts
- bed, bath and table
- sports and leisure
- computers and accessories

Business interpretation:

- The top categories combine volume and ticket strength.
- Some categories generate high revenue through high order volume, while others stand out because of higher average ticket.
- Payment behavior is dominated by credit card, followed by boleto.

Potential actions:

- Prioritize commercial campaigns in categories with strong volume and healthy ticket.
- Monitor categories with high GMV but lower satisfaction.
- Segment payment strategy by ticket size and purchase behavior.

## Logistics

Delivery is one of the most important pages in the report because it connects operations with customer perception.

Key readings:

- Average delivery time is around 12.6 days.
- Median delivery time is around 10.2 days.
- Around 8.1% of delivered orders were late.
- Late orders have a materially lower average review score.

Potential actions:

- Monitor late delivery rate by state.
- Investigate regions with high delay concentration.
- Compare seller origin and customer destination to understand freight and SLA pressure.

## Customers And Sellers

The customer and seller pages show strong geographic concentration.

Key readings:

- São Paulo is the main state for both demand and seller origin.
- Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais are important demand centers.
- Seller revenue is heavily concentrated in Southeast and South states.

Potential actions:

- Use geographic concentration to optimize logistics partnerships.
- Investigate states with high demand but lower seller presence.
- Compare seller origin and customer destination to reduce freight cost and delivery time.

## Satisfaction

The satisfaction page connects reviews with operational performance.

Key readings:

- Most reviews are positive, with a large share of 5-star evaluations.
- Reviews with 1 or 2 stars represent the most relevant group for root-cause analysis.
- Delivery delay is associated with a clear drop in average rating.
- Category-level review score required special modeling to connect product category with review records.

Potential actions:

- Track low reviews by category, seller and delivery status.
- Use review comments to classify complaint themes.
- Prioritize experience improvements in categories with relevant volume and lower average score.

## Portfolio Value

This project demonstrates the ability to:

- Translate raw marketplace data into business questions.
- Build a semantic model suitable for cross-domain analysis.
- Write DAX measures that handle context and relationship challenges.
- Design a professional multi-page BI product.
- Connect commercial, logistics and satisfaction metrics into one analytical narrative.
